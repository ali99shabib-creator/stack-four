Stack Four Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Stack Four is a local strategy game designed to run on the user's device.

Personal Data

Stack Four does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Accounts and Login

The app does not require account registration or login.

Location, Contacts, Camera, and Microphone

The app does not access location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device information.

Analytics and Advertising

The current version of Stack Four does not include advertising SDKs or analytics services.

Internet Connection

The game can be played locally and does not require an internet connection for normal gameplay.

Children's Privacy

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Privacy Rights

Since Stack Four does not collect personal data, there is no personal data stored by the app to access, correct, or delete.

Changes to This Policy

If future versions add features that collect or process data, this privacy policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact the developer through the app's official store listing.
