Stack Four Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Stack Four is a local strategy board game designed to run on the user's device.

Personal Data

Stack Four does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Accounts and Login

The app does not require users to create an account or log in.

Location, Contacts, Camera, and Microphone

Stack Four does not access the user's location, contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device information.

Analytics and Advertising

The current version of Stack Four does not contain advertising SDKs or analytics services.

Internet Connection

The game can be played locally and does not require an internet connection for normal gameplay.

Local Multiplayer

The local two-player mode operates on the same device and does not transmit player information to external servers.

Game Preferences

Settings such as language, difficulty, sound preferences, and piece colors may be stored locally on the user's device. These settings are not transmitted to the developer.

Children's Privacy

Stack Four does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Data Subject Rights

Because Stack Four does not collect or store personal data, there is no personal information held by the developer to access, correct, transfer, or delete.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If a future version of Stack Four introduces features that collect or process personal information, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those features are released.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, users may contact the developer through the official Stack Four app listing.
